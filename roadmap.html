New to DSA?

How to use this roadmap

Follow it top to bottom. Each stage only uses ideas from the stages before it.

1

Know one language

Loops, if/else, functions and arrays in Java, C++ or Python. That's all you need for Stage 1.

2

Easy first, always

Every pattern lists Easy, then Medium, then Hard. Do 3–4 Easy before touching a Medium.

3

Struggle 30 minutes

Stuck after 30–45 minutes? Take one hint, not the solution. Then try again.

4

One pattern at a time

Read the pattern's guide, solve its problems, then move on. 1–2 problems a day beats 20 on a Sunday.

5

Revisit after a week

Re-solve anything you needed a hint for. That's when the pattern actually sticks.
